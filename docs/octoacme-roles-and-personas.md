# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality assurance and testing strategy. They define test plans, coordinate testing efforts, and ensure acceptance criteria are met before features move to production.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy and approach during planning phase
- Create and maintain test plans, test cases, and QA checklists
- Execute manual and automated testing; coordinate with developers on test automation
- Validate acceptance criteria and sign-off on feature readiness
- Identify and triage defects; work with developers on root cause analysis
- Participate in pre-release smoke tests and production monitoring
- Report quality metrics and trends

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality deliverables that meet acceptance criteria
- Reduce defects reaching production
- Enable confident, low-risk releases

### Typical Communication
- Quality gates and test status in standups and weekly syncs
- Defect reports and severity tracking in project board
- Pre-release readiness checklists and sign-off

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Works with Developers**: Collaborates on test automation, defect triage, and acceptance criteria validation
- **Works with Project Managers**: Reports on quality metrics and participates in risk management for quality-related issues
- **Works with Product Managers**: Validates that features meet acceptance criteria and user expectations
- **Works with Technical Lead/Architect**: Ensures test coverage aligns with architectural decisions and quality standards

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide architectural decisions, design, and technical risk mitigation. They ensure solutions are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with system standards.

### Responsibilities
- Lead technical design and architecture reviews
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Estimate technical effort and feasibility during planning
- Mentor developers and guide code quality standards
- Support capacity planning and dependency identification
- Participate in retrospectives to capture technical learnings

### Goals
- Deliver technically sound, scalable solutions
- Minimize technical debt and rework
- Build team capability through mentorship

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and RFCs (Request for Comments)
- Risk identification in planning and weekly syncs
- Code review guidance and architectural decisions
- Post-incident technical analysis

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Works with Developers**: Provides technical guidance, mentorship, and design review feedback
- **Works with Project Managers**: Identifies and communicates technical dependencies and risks
- **Works with QA/Testing Lead**: Ensures test strategy aligns with technical architecture
- **Works with Sponsor/Executive**: Escalates significant technical risks and resource constraints

---

## Sponsor / Executive

### Role Summary
Sponsors provide business context, resource commitment, and executive decision-making. They serve as the escalation point for business-impacting issues and remove organizational blockers.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charter and business case
- Allocate resources and authorize budget
- Serve as Level 3 escalation for critical issues
- Review and approve high-level plans and milestones
- Provide stakeholder alignment and cross-organizational support
- Review release announcements and outcomes

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver on strategic business objectives
- Remove organizational barriers to execution
- Maintain stakeholder confidence and alignment

### Typical Communication
- Project initiation and approval gates
- Monthly stakeholder updates and milestone reviews
- Escalation and issue resolution on critical blockers
- Release announcements and outcome reviews

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Works with Project Managers**: Receives escalations on critical blockers and participates in decision gates
- **Works with Product Managers**: Aligns on business objectives and strategic priorities
- **Works with Stakeholder Manager/Communications Lead**: Approves key communications and stakeholder updates

---

## Stakeholder Manager / Communications Lead

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Managers coordinate communication with external stakeholders, manage expectations, and ensure transparency across functional areas.

### Responsibilities
- Identify stakeholder groups and communication needs during initiation
- Draft and distribute weekly status updates and risk communications
- Coordinate release announcements and deployment windows
- Manage stakeholder feedback and concerns
- Facilitate escalation communication between team and sponsors
- Archive decisions and communications for reference

### Goals
- Maintain alignment and transparency across stakeholders
- Reduce surprises and escalations through proactive communication
- Build trust through consistent, clear messaging

### Typical Communication
- Weekly stakeholder status templates and distribution
- Release notes and deployment announcements
- Risk and incident communications
- Decision logs and retrospective summaries

### Interactions with Other Roles
- **Works with Project Managers**: Coordinates on status updates, risk communications, and escalations
- **Works with Sponsor/Executive**: Ensures key communications are approved and distributed effectively
- **Works with Product Managers**: Aligns on roadmap and feature announcements
- **Works with QA/Testing Lead**: Communicates release readiness and quality status to stakeholders
